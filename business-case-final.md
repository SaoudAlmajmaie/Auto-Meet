# Business Case --- AutoMeet

## 1. Problem / Opportunity

Information about local car shows and automotive events is often spread
across social media pages, car club websites, and community event
listings. This makes it difficult and time-consuming for car enthusiasts
to find accurate information about upcoming events in one place.

## 2. Proposed Solution

AutoMeet is a web application that provides one organized place for
users to discover local car shows and automotive events. Users can
browse upcoming events and select an individual event to view details
such as the event name, date, location, description, vehicle type, and
admission information. A simple bookmarking feature can also help users
keep track of events they are interested in attending.

## 6. Priority & Urgency

Local event information continues to be distributed across multiple
platforms. Developing AutoMeet now provides an opportunity to test a
simpler, centralized approach within the course timeframe. Waiting would
leave users relying on scattered sources and delay the opportunity to
evaluate the concept.

## 7. Recommendation

Proceed with Option B and develop the AutoMeet prototype because it
addresses a clear user need and is feasible within the available course
timeframe.
